Type 2 Cell

Phase 2

Type 2 Cells are a type of cell that has two layers, a top and a bottom which each act as their own seperate type 1 cells.
Type 2 Cells have two properties: upperBit and lowerBit.
upperBit and lowerBit are booleans that can be on (1/true) or off (0/false).
You can see the state of the upperBit and lowerBit of a type 1 cell through it's colour which will either be black (both off), red (only upperBit on), blue (only lowerBit on) or white (both on).
